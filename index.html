FAQ

Common questions

Have a question that isn't here? Send it with the form below.

How often should my chimney be cleaned?

The national standard (NFPA 211) calls for an inspection once a year. Cleaning is needed when buildup reaches about 1/8 inch, which for many wood-burning homes means once a year as well.

What's the difference between a Level 1 and Level 2 inspection?

Level 1 covers the parts you can reach and see: firebox, damper, crown, cap and exterior. Level 2 adds a camera scan of the inside of the flue and is recommended when you buy or sell a home, change fuel type, or after a chimney fire.

Will it make a mess in my house?

No. We lay drop cloths, seal the fireplace opening, and run a HEPA vacuum while we work.

Do you work in winter?

Yes. Cleanings and inspections run all year. Masonry repairs depend on temperature, so we schedule those for days when mortar can cure properly.

My chimney has a funny smell. Is it dangerous?

The smell itself usually comes from creosote, which is flammable. It's worth having it cleaned and inspected before your next fire.

How much does chimney cleaning cost in the Twin Cities?

Most local companies charge about $189 to $249 for a standard sweep. Our fall special is $179 for a sweep plus a Level 1 inspection through December 31. Repairs are quoted in writing after we inspect.

Do I really need a chimney cap?

A cap keeps rain, snow, birds and squirrels out of the flue. It's one of the cheapest ways to prevent water damage.
